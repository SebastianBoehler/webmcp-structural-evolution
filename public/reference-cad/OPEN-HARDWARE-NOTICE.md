# OpenDrone reference CAD

The following display assets are exact-revision derivatives of official OpenDrone release STEP assemblies:

- `opendrone-openfc-lite-rev3.3.glb` derives from [OpenFC-Lite rev3.3](https://github.com/OpenDrone-hw/OpenFC-Lite/releases/tag/rev3.3), SHA-256 `ac8cb93a42d54cff67e15b6442b4eb74c003015a06e943cb0222cc981303669d`.
- `opendrone-openesc-30x30-rev3.3.glb` derives from [OpenESC-30x30 rev3.3](https://github.com/OpenDrone-hw/OpenESC-30x30/releases/tag/rev3.3), SHA-256 `dadded39478f0c7525d3b89722fa7fa57fb794cea374832f197d407bee34e6e3`.

Both upstream hardware designs are licensed under CERN-OHL-S-2.0. Their complete corresponding source, KiCad project, notices, and original STEP assemblies are available at the linked repositories. The GLBs retain the source B-rep tessellation, are recentered without dimensional scaling, and include source, checksum, unit, licence, and processing metadata in glTF `extras`.
